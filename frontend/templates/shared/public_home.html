Help

Frequently asked questions

Who can use FieldCRM?

Authorized Mainstreet staff receive role-based access for field capture, review, approval, administration, and audit work.

Can I work offline?

The Android app can retain supported field data locally and synchronize it when connectivity returns. Server confirmation remains authoritative.

How do I install the APK?

Download the signed APK on an Android 8.0+ device, approve installation from this source when prompted, and open FieldCRM.

Where can I get support?

Contact your Mainstreet system administrator or internal technology support team.
