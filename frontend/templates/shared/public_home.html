Useful answers

Before you begin.

FieldCRM is an internal operations platform for authorised Mainstreet staff.

Who can use FieldCRM?+

Authorised Mainstreet staff receive role-based access for field capture, review, approval, administration, and audit work.

Can field teams work offline?+

The Android app can retain supported field data locally and synchronise when connectivity returns. Server confirmation remains authoritative.

How is a decision traced?+

Workflow events, audit history, documents, reasons, and responsible actors remain connected to the application dossier.

Where can staff get support?+

Contact your Mainstreet system administrator or internal technology support team.
