Structured origination

Defined stages. Evidence carried forward.

  1. Open

    Customer, product, requested amount, purpose and responsible officer establish the application record.

  2. Capture

    Identity, business, financial, guarantor, collateral, location, visit and supporting details complete the dossier.

  3. Validate

    Server-managed fields, documents, checks, limits, readiness rules and the applicable configuration version govern submission.

  4. Advance

    Recorded recommendations and authorised workflow actions move the same dossier to the next responsible desk.
