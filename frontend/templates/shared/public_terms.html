Terms for using FieldCRM

FieldCRM is an internal credit-operations platform provided for authorized Mainstreet Microfinance Bank personnel. By accessing it, you agree to use it only for approved business activities and in accordance with Mainstreet policies.

Authorized access

Access is limited to approved users. You must use your assigned account, protect authentication credentials, complete required device security checks, and immediately report suspected unauthorized access.

Acceptable use

You may use FieldCRM only to perform assigned credit intake, verification, review, approval, administration, monitoring, and audit duties. You must not bypass role permissions, alter records without authority, introduce harmful software, or use platform information for personal purposes.

Information and decisions

Users must enter accurate information, verify supporting evidence, and follow approved credit policies. FieldCRM supports workflows and record keeping; it does not replace professional judgment, required approvals, or applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

Confidentiality and records

Customer, guarantor, staff, document, and transaction information is confidential. It must not be copied, disclosed, downloaded, or retained outside approved Mainstreet systems except where expressly authorized. Relevant actions may be logged and reviewed.

Availability and changes

Mainstreet may maintain, update, suspend, or restrict the platform where reasonably required for security, operations, compliance, or service continuity. Features and these terms may be updated through approved organizational processes.

Support and enforcement

Report technical issues through the official Mainstreet support channel. Misuse may result in access suspension, internal disciplinary action, or other action permitted by policy and law.

FieldCRM supports Mainstreet's field credit operations.
