Privacy

FieldCRM privacy information

FieldCRM is an internal credit-operations platform used by authorized Mainstreet Microfinance Bank personnel. This page provides a plain-language overview; approved Mainstreet policies and customer notices remain authoritative.

Information processed

The platform processes staff account information and loan-processing information supplied for applications, identity and credit checks, supporting documents, field visits, decisions, disbursement, repayment monitoring, and audit records.

Why it is used

Information is used to evaluate and administer credit, complete required verification and compliance work, coordinate authorized reviews, communicate about applications, and preserve evidence of decisions.

Access and security

Access is limited by authenticated staff role and organizational scope. FieldCRM records relevant workflow actions and uses protected storage and transport controls. Staff must use only their assigned account and approved devices.

Storage and service providers

Authorized infrastructure and service providers may process information to provide hosting, document storage, communications, identity verification, AML screening, credit reporting, and operational support, subject to Mainstreet’s approved arrangements.

Requests and support

Customers and staff should direct privacy questions, access requests, corrections, or complaints through the official Mainstreet Microfinance Bank support and data-protection channels supplied by the bank.

FieldCRM supports Mainstreet’s field credit operations.
