The Android workspace displays the canonical label “Relationship Officer”; the database/web role covered here is `account_officer`.

## Summary

- High: **3**
- Medium: **6**
- Low: **1**

The most consequential gap is the absence of a role-owned OCR review queue, which prevents officers from discovering all files awaiting OCR correction from Android.
